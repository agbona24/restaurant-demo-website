Our Story

A little corner of home in Lekki

Lekki Bites started as a small kitchen on Admiralty Way with one promise: no shortcuts on flavour. Every pot of jollof, every bowl of egusi, is cooked slow and seasoned generously, the way it's done at home.

Today we're a favourite stop for Lekki families, Island professionals on lunch break, and anyone craving real Naija food without the wait.
